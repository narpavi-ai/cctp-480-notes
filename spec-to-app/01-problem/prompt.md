I have a rough idea for a small app: **[one-sentence idea]**.

Before I build anything, help me get specific. Ask me follow-up questions
one at a time until we can answer all of these clearly:

1. **Who** specifically has this problem? (Not "everyone" — a specific
   person or small group.)
2. **What do they do today** instead, even if it's manual or messy?
3. **Where does that break down** for them — what's the actual pain?
4. **What would "better" look like**, in their words if possible?

Once we've answered all four, turn the answers into a short spec:

- A list of 3-5 screens the app needs
- The core flow a user takes through those screens
- One or two things that should explicitly be **out of scope** for a first
  version
